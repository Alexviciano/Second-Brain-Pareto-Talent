# Your Second Brain: Quick Guide for Kasim

## What it is
Your second brain is a folder in Google Drive called **kasim-aslam-second-brain**. It holds short notes about you, your companies, your people, your offers and your ideas. Claude reads that folder.

That means you stop re-explaining yourself. Claude already knows who Ivan is, what the Phoenix workshop costs, and how you think about delegation. When it answers, it can tell you which note the answer came from.

You never need to open the folders yourself. They're organized for Claude, not for you.

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| 00 Context | Who you are, your current focus, your offers and prices, your morning brief |
| 01 Companies | Pareto Talent, The Daily Sigh, your workshops, your agencies, your business history |
| 02 People | One note per person: Ivan, Nuno, Greg, clients, the Pareto team |
| 03 Ideas & Content | Your frameworks: delegation, AI leadership, the extinction series |
| 04 Events | Workshops and lives, with dates and prices |
| 07–08 Inboxes | New calls and documents, processed and filed by Claude |
| 06 Personal | Empty. Only filled if you ask for it |

## How to ask it questions
1. Open Claude and go to the project **Kasim Aslam: Second Brain**.
2. Ask the way you'd ask your EA. You can type or use voice.
3. When accuracy matters, end with: **"Which file did you use?"**

Questions that work well:
- "What's the date and price of the Phoenix workshop?"
- "Before my call with Markus, remind me who he is and what he's said about Pareto."
- "Who runs finance and client success at Pareto Talent?"
- "Draft a LinkedIn post in my voice about what not to delegate."
- "Which Daily Sigh guests talked about building AI systems?"

Two tips:
- If an answer says **[to be filled]**, the brain doesn't know yet. Give Claude the answer and say "save this."
- If something is wrong, say "That's wrong, it's X. Fix it in the second brain."

## How to add new information
**The one rule: don't drop files into the folders yourself.** Give them to Claude and say **"save this to my second brain."** Claude picks the right folder, formats the note and links it to related notes. A file you drop in by hand is invisible to that system and gets lost.

| How | Example |
|---|---|
| **Say it** (voice is fastest) | "Save to my second brain: the company I run with Nuno is called …" |
| **Hand it a file** | Drop in a transcript, PDF, contract or export: "Process this and save it to my second brain." |
| **Let it pull** (once your apps are connected) | "Go through my last 10 Fathom calls and save the key points, not the full transcripts." |

**Worth adding:** calls, decisions, promises you made, new offers or prices, and new people.
**Leave out:** spam, whole inbox dumps, and anything private. This folder is shared with your team.

I keep the brain updated day to day. You only need to add things when something comes up in your head or on a call.

## Your first 5 minutes
- [ ] Ask Claude: "What do you know about me? List the files you used."
- [ ] Correct anything that's wrong.
- [ ] Fill three gaps: Which company was your exit? What are your agencies called? What company do you run with Nuno?
- [ ] Record a one-minute voice note on your top priority this quarter and ask Claude to save it.
