# AI as an Economic Mass Extinction (article series, Sep 2026)

## Part 0: "Terror and Excitement" (~2026-09-21)
He has felt terror and excitement "every. single. day." since AI arrived: terrified of being left behind, irrelevant, obsolete, and excited enough "to burst." Being a "neurotic, armchair doomsday prepper," he asked ChatGPT for historical analogies. He found no human-economic precedent, so he went further back than human history. "You're not going to like the answer."

## Part 1: The extinction event (~2026-09-22)
The closest analogy for AI (according to AI) isn't a technological revolution. It's a **mass extinction**. AI doesn't wipe out humanity, but it wipes out most businesses (75%+) in a very short timeframe, because it radically destabilizes the environment.
