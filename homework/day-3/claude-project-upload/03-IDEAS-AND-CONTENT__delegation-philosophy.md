# Delegation Philosophy

## Core idea: delegate the destination, not the steps
*"If your team only does as well as you would've, you delegated wrong."* (Instagram, Jun 23, 2026)
- Step-by-step instructions cap people at what you already knew. "The instructions are just you, frozen on the day you wrote them."
- Give people the destination and let them "read the terrain rather than the page." Your best people will find routes you never would.
- The world changes after instructions are written. "A map is the past, folded."
