# AI Leadership Framework

*"Founders: Stop trying to 'learn AI'."* (LinkedIn/Facebook, ~2026-09-18)

- You don't need to be the best AI user in your company. You need to be **the best leader of people who use AI**.
- **Leverage math:** you using AI = 1 person leveraged; you leading many people using AI = many people leveraged, and leverage compounds.
  - Old world: 10 people × 1 = **10**
  - AI-native company: 10 × 5 = **50**
  - AI-native + strong leadership: 10 × 5 × organizational leverage = **1,000+**
- "The day you don't need people will be the very same day the economy doesn't need you."
- The opportunity is "a classic land grab".

1. The First Principles of AI
2. Context Engineering
3. AI-Led Execution
