# CLAUDE.md: Kasim Aslam's Second Brain

This folder is **Kasim Aslam's second brain**. Treat it as the source of truth about Kasim, his companies, his people and his ideas.

## How to navigate
1. Start at [[_MOC]] (root map), then open the `_MOC.md` of the folder you need.
2. Read a file's YAML frontmatter first. Open the full file only if it is relevant.
3. Follow `[[wikilinks]]` to related files. Don't load everything at once.
4. When you answer a question, **name the file(s) you used** (e.g. "Source: 02-PEOPLE/ivan-bunin.md").

## How to add information
- New material (transcripts, PDFs, posts, exports) comes in through Claude, never by dragging files into folders by hand.
- Convert it to Markdown, give it frontmatter (see [[frontmatter-standards]]), save it in the right folder, link it to related files, and update that folder's `_MOC.md`.
- Save **processed insights**, not raw dumps. Raw exports go in `10-SOURCES/` only if they are worth keeping.
- Never invent facts. Unknowns are written as `[to be filled]`. Uncertain facts get `confidence: likely` or `to-verify`.

## Conventions
- kebab-case filenames, ISO dates (YYYY-MM-DD), numeric folder prefixes.
- One concept per file (atomic), heavily interlinked, tagged.
- Do not write personal or sensitive information into this brain unless Kasim explicitly asks.
- Do not modify this brain during unrelated chats unless asked to.
